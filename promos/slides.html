Reconciliation

MYOB books
what the
customer paid

The payload never states a total. My adds it up anyway, and refuses to push if it's out by more than a cent.

  • Checked again against what MYOB actually booked, which catches a tax code mapped to the wrong rate
  • Lines come from Commerce's own arithmetic, so third-party surcharges are never dropped

The sync ledger

Nothing is
entered twice

One invoice per order, one payment per transaction. Retry all you like.

  • Each row is claimed before the request, not after
  • A row left pending asks MYOB whether the invoice landed before posting it again
  • The order reference rides on every invoice, so a human can always match them up

Refunds

Refunds
become credit
notes

A Commerce refund becomes a MYOB credit note: an invoice with negative amounts.

  • Leave the credit on the customer's account, or record the money going back out
  • Partial refunds are credited at the tax treatment the order was charged
  • A second refund on the same order is CR2-, never a clash
